FAQ.

Last updated · Feb 2026

How do I know what size to order?
Our garments are cut boxy and true to size. The Vol. 03 Heavyweight Tee sits close to the body without clinging. See the size guide for centimetre-accurate measurements.
When will you restock a sold-out piece?
Most silhouettes are made in one run. Occasionally, if demand supports it, we restock in the next volume — usually four months later. Sign up for The List to be first to know.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes — we ship to 42 countries. Duties are calculated at checkout so there are no surprises at your door.
What is your returns window?
Fourteen days from delivery. Garment must be unworn with the original tags. Full details on the returns page.
Can I exchange a garment for a different size?
Yes — email care@lapdog.co within seven days and we will send a prepaid label.
How do I care for a LapDog garment?
Cold wash inside out. Never tumble dry. Iron reverse. Written care instructions are printed on the inside tag of every piece.
Do you offer gift cards?
Yes — available in denominations from ₹2,000 to ₹25,000. Delivered by email.
Do you have a physical store?
No physical store. We host regular studio events in Mumbai — see the Community page.
How does the pay-in-3 option work?
At checkout, choose 'Pay Later · 3 Instalments'. You will pay one-third at checkout and the balance in two monthly instalments. No interest. No fees.
Can I collaborate with LapDog?
We work with a small number of houses each year. Please write to hello@lapdog.co with a brief.
