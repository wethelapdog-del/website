The house

A small quiet studio.

LapDog began as an argument between two friends about a t-shirt. It is now a wardrobe philosophy — quietly loud, obsessively made, small on purpose.

Method.

We work in volumes — twelve pieces at a time. Every piece is cut, sewn, washed and photographed before it lists. When the volume sells through, it stays sold through until the next one.

No sales. No influencer seeding. No mall-scale mass. If a garment is worth making, it is worth waiting for.

Materials.

280 to 320 gsm compact-spun cotton. Certified OEKO-TEX. Cut in-house, sewn a short train ride away. Nothing fancy — just correct.

Our promise

Nothing unnecessary.

No trims that will not last four years. No prints that will fade. No sizes we cannot pattern properly. When we do not know how to make it, we do not make it.

Our people

Small team.

Six people. One studio. A shared calendar for pattern reviews. A weekly cup of the same coffee. We have never met an org chart we liked.
